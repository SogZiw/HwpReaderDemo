@hwp.js/parser 0.0.4-alpha.0
  https://github.com/hahnlee/hwp.js
  https://registry.npmjs.org/@hwp.js/parser/-/parser-0.0.4-alpha.0.tgz
  Copyright Han Lee and other contributors. Apache-2.0.
  package/LICENSE is preserved as hwp.js/PARSER-LICENSE.
  Modifications: ESM imports use the local CFB/pako browser globals; exports are
  assigned to HWPParser. Inflate uses an indirection for the demo's expansion
  limit. The source map URL was removed. Record compatibility fixes are applied
  reproducibly by tools/patch-parser.py: ten-level numbering starts in 5.0.3,
  storage binary extensions, bit-mask/composite fills with bounded extensions,
  numeric effect codes, extended-char record bounds, and trailing caption/list
  paragraph preservation. Both adapted and patched SHA-256 are pinned.
  Original distribution sources are preserved in hwp.js/parser-index.js.map.
